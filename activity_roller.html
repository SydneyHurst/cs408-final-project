Item Roller

Characters not listed as Healthy will incur a 10% penalty to their roll. This works by removing 10 from the rolled number to determine the rarity of the item.

This roller only rolls Common, Uncommon, and Rare items. Theoretically it could be edited to roll specific items within those rarities.
