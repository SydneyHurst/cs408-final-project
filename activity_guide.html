Items are cool

Use the item roller here:

This rolls items ranging from common to rare. In a more complex roller it would roll items based on the rarity. Ive made such rollers in (believe it or not) google sheets.

The rolling odds are: 1-50 common, 51-80 uncommon and 81-100 rare (when rolling 1-100).

I used these numbers in an effort to (on the very off chance that someone who may see this plays the game i admin for) keep certain rolling numbers secret, otherwise i would have used the same number table and used the roller beyond this project.
